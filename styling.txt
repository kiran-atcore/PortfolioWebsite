1. CORE DESIGN AESTHETIC & THEME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Theme: Cybernetic / Aerospace / High-Tech Futuristic Dark Mode
- Philosophy: Obsidian/void dark backdrops with vivid neon cyan & magenta accents,
  glassmorphism (heavy backdrop-blur + semi-transparent dark tint), crisp technical
  borders, subtle glow drop-shadows, and micro-animations.
